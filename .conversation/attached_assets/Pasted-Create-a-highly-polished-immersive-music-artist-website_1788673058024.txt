Create a highly polished, immersive music artist website based on the attached visual moodboard.

The core concept is:

THE ARTIST IS A TV CHANNEL.

Visitors aren't simply browsing an artist's website — they are tuning into the artist's channel.

The visual identity should evoke the playful, rebellious, colorful energy of late-90s/2000s cartoon television and animated network idents, while remaining completely original and not directly copying Cartoon Network logos, characters, trademarks, or specific show designs.

The site should feel like "a cartoon channel for music" — playful, weird, nostalgic, slightly chaotic, but still sophisticated enough to function as a professional music artist website.

The design should feel handmade and imperfect, rather than like a clean corporate website.

Use asymmetry and unexpected positioning. Elements can slightly overlap, rotate, or break outside conventional containers.

However, maintain excellent UX and readability.

Typography

Typography is extremely important.

Use a combination of:

A bold, chunky, playful display typeface for major headings.
A hand-drawn/marker-style typeface for secondary text.
A clean sans-serif for functional UI elements.

Large headlines should sometimes look like cut-out letters assembled from different pieces of paper, similar to a ransom-note/collage alphabet.

Do NOT use one font for everything.

SITE EXPERIENCE

The website should open like a television broadcast.

When the visitor first arrives, create a very short channel-ident animation.

Something like:

STATIC → CHANNEL SIGNAL → CH. 01 → ARTIST NAME → WEBSITE

Then transition smoothly into the homepage.

Do not make the intro annoying or excessively long. It should be approximately 1–2 seconds and allow the visitor to immediately enter the site.

Include a subtle "SKIP INTRO" option.

SECTION 05 — MEET THE CHARACTER

MOST IMPORTANT CREATIVE DIRECTION

The website should make someone think:

"Wait... this isn't an artist website. This is a TV channel."

It should feel nostalgic without being outdated.

It should feel cartoonish without looking childish.

It should feel chaotic without becoming messy.

It should feel experimental without sacrificing usability.

The artist's music is the content.
The website is the channel.

Build the entire experience around that idea.
